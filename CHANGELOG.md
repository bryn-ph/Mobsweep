# Changelog

## v0.1.0-beta

First beta.

- Targets and marks the mobs your focused or tracked quest needs.
- Tracking panel with a button you can bind. With Tracking on it glows and alerts when a quest mob is near, and again when it's marked.
- Also kept as a `Quarry` macro for your bars.
- Loot quests too, if you have Questie.
- Pick the raid marker, or turn marking off.
- Set the Tag key from the settings window.
- Full or minimal Tracking panel. Minimal shows its details when you hover it. Right-click the button to turn Tracking on or off, drag it to move it.
- Choose the alert sound, or none.
